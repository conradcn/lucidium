## How to agree

Confirm the checkbox in the pull request template:

> - [ ] I have read and agree to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

Checking that box, or stating **"I have read the CLA and I agree to it"** in a
comment on your pull request, constitutes your agreement to this document for
that Contribution and every Contribution you make to the Project afterwards.
It has the same effect as a signature.

If you are agreeing on behalf of an employer or other legal entity, say so in
the pull request and confirm that you are authorized to bind that entity.
